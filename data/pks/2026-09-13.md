# PKS MPI Dresden — Week of 14–20 September 2026

A lean week, but a strong one: two Hubbard/quantum-gas seminars on the same afternoon, a topological-order theory talk, and a computational-neuroscience colloquium with a coding-theory angle.

## Monday, 14 September

**15:30 — What to throw away? How brains make efficient codes**
Dr Stephanie Palmer (University of Chicago)
Worth it for the compression/efficient-coding framing of neural data — the information-theoretic and dimensionality-reduction machinery transfers directly to analyzing high-dimensional physics and simulation data.
[Event link](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98368)

## Wednesday, 16 September — *Hubbard model double feature*

**15:30 — IMPRS Seminar: Quantum gas microscopy of Hubbard systems**
Peter Schauss (University of Hamburg)
A current snapshot of single-site-resolved cold-atom Hubbard experiments — the cleanest available quantum simulators of strongly correlated lattice physics.
[Event link](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98810)

**16:45 — IMPRS Seminar: Probing pairing spectra using Feshbach spectroscopy of the attractive Hubbard model**
Luke Staszewski (MPIPKS, Dresden)
Directly follows the previous talk; connects attractive-Hubbard pairing to measurable Feshbach spectra — relevant if you care about superconductivity from a cold-atom angle.
[Event link](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98811)

---

## Thursday, 17 September

**15:00 — New connections between Laughlin, BCS, and Moore-Read states and classical 2D Coulomb gases**
Inti Sodemann (Institute for Theoretical Physics, University of Leipzig)
Topological order meets plasma/Coulomb-gas mappings — a theory talk likely to reframe trial wavefunctions in statistical-mechanics language, useful well beyond fractional quantum Hall.
[Event link](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98943)

---

### Quick take
If you attend only one event, make it **Sodemann on Thursday** — the Coulomb-gas reformulation is the kind of cross-domain mapping that tends to generate new methods. If your work is cold-atom adjacent, block out **Wednesday afternoon entirely** and take both IMPRS seminars back to back.

## 9 月 16 日（周三）

### ⚛️ IMPRS Seminar: Quantum gas microscopy of Hubbard systems
- **时间**：15:30

- **推荐理由**：量子气体显微镜是研究 Hubbard 模型量子模拟的核心实验手段，直接关联强关联电子系统与冷原子模拟。对凝聚态理论与量子模拟方向的研究者优先推荐。
- **链接**：[IMPRS Seminar: Quantum gas microscopy of Hubbard systems](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98810)

### 🔬 IMPRS Seminar: Probing pairing spectra using Feshbach spectroscopy of the attractive Hubbard model

- **推荐理由**：紧接上一场，聚焦吸引 Hubbard 模型中的配对谱，涉及超导配对机制与 Feshbach 谱学。与前一讲座形成"实验—理论"互补，适合对超导、配对物理感兴趣者连听。本地讲者，便于会后交流。
- **链接**：[Probing pairing spectra using Feshbach spectroscopy of the attractive Hubbard model](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98811)

## 9 月 17 日（周四）

### 🌊 New connections between Laughlin, BCS, and Moore-Read states and classical 2D Coulomb gases
- **时间**：15:00
- **主讲**：Inti Sodemann（Institute for Theoretical Physics, University of Leipzig）
