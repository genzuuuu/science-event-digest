# Weekly Digest: Condensed Matter & Complex Systems – Dresden (July 13–19, 2026)

## Monday, July 13

- **03:30 PM – Talk: [Reaction-diffusion systems that learn, reproduce, and adapt](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_95586)**  
  Olivier Rivoire (CNRS, ESPCI) presents how non-equilibrium patterns in reaction-diffusion systems can exhibit learning and evolutionary dynamics — a key topic for complex systems and active matter.

- **All day – Workshop start: [High-Dimensional Computation in Soft Matter](https://www.pks.mpg.de/hdcsm26)**  
  A week-long workshop (starts today) on high-dimensional statistical mechanics and computation in soft materials, organized by Izaak Neri and Pablo Sartori. Strong ML/physics overlap.

## Tuesday, July 14

- **11:00 AM – Talk: [A rigidity transition terminates convergence flows in zebrafish embryos](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_99119)**  
  Swetha Nagarajan (TIFR Mumbai) on rigidity transitions in biological tissues — directly relevant to jamming, active matter, and nonequilibrium phase transitions.

## Wednesday, July 15

- **03:30 PM – IMPRS Seminar: [State-Specific vs Time-Dependent DFT for Organic Electronics Materials](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_99122)**  
  Jan-Michael Mewes (beeOLED GmbH) compares electronic structure methods for organic electronics — key for materials modelling and quantum chemistry.

- **04:45 PM – IMPRS Seminar: [Spectral Detective Work: Quantum Chemistry Meets Reactive-Probe Experiments](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_99123)**  
  Jan Polena (UCT Prague) on combining quantum chemistry with reactive-probe experiments — important for spectroscopy and molecular electronics.

## Thursday, July 16

- **09:00 AM – Talk: [Hydrodynamic simulations with a Boltzmann lattice automaton](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98753)**  
  Julian Gross (TU Dresden) on automated lattice Boltzmann methods for hydrodynamics — a computational method directly relevant to fluid dynamics and soft matter.

- **03:00 PM – Talk: [Integrating Floquet and reservoir engineering to prepare correlated quantum states](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98191)**  
  Francesco Petiziol (TU Berlin) on periodic driving (Floquet) and reservoir engineering for quantum state preparation — core to quantum condensed matter.

- **04:30 PM – Quantum Dynamics Seminar: [Open system approaches to ultrafast spectroscopy and optical control](https://www.pks.mpg.de/events/talks#eventId_98155)**  
  Dr. Frank Schlawin (Universität Hamburg) on open quantum systems for ultrafast spectroscopy — overlaps strongly with quantum optics, nanophotonics, and non-equilibrium condensed matter.
